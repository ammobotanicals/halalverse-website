FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions — Halal Verse

What does Halal Verse actually do?

We guide businesses through the entire halal certification journey — from initial readiness assessment to certification and beyond. Rather than just handling paperwork, we help you build genuine, sustainable halal compliance that opens access to the halal market.

Who is Halal Verse for?

We work with food and beverage businesses of all sizes, from local UMKM in Bali to companies preparing for export. If your product needs to meet halal standards, we can help you get there.

Do I need to know anything about halal certification before starting?

Not at all. Many of our clients start with little prior knowledge. We begin with a readiness assessment and guide you step by step, explaining each requirement in plain terms.

How long does halal certification take?

Timelines vary depending on your product complexity, supply chain, and current readiness. After your initial assessment, we provide a realistic timeline tailored to your business.

Is Halal Verse officially recognized?

Yes. Our facilitators and supervisors are registered with BPJPH. PT. Halal Verse Indonesia is a legally registered entity, and we operate as a registered MSME under the Bali Provincial Department of Cooperatives and MSMEs.

Do you support businesses after certification?

Yes. Halal compliance is ongoing, not a one-time event. We provide guidance on maintaining your halal system so your certification stays valid and credible.
